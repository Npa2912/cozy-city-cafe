# River city extension plan

Baseline commit: `a2f6184`. Existing base: approximately X −65…65,
Z −103…47 in project world coordinates, not surveyed real-world area. The sea
lies east at X 66. The café/core origin, annex, eastern residential street,
promenade, pier, lighthouse, fish and day/night system stay in place.

The empty western hill at X approximately −65…−22 is replaced by blocks; the
new urban envelope extends west to X −145 and north to Z −108, ending at the
foreground quay Z 50. A curved canal around X −76 runs through this extension
and opens into connected sea at both map edges. West streets at X −124 and
−101 and an eastern avenue at X −47 connect through road bridges at Z 22 and
−100 and 42; a separate arched pedestrian bridge lies at Z −40. The northern and southern cross
streets at Z −100 and 42 join the retained core road at X 4.

The extension adds 35 low/mid-rise lots, eight shop-base towers, a clock hall and a conservatory (45 buildings). Buildings face the planned streets. Five frontage bands alternate gables,
rounded corners, twin dormers, rooftop terraces, shops and three/four-storey
residences. Setback towers occupy deeper lots rather than being confined to one
edge. River walks, pocket plazas, a conservatory and civic clock building fill
the blocks between existing and new streets. Boundaries and footprints are
checked in world coordinates; this does not claim a calibrated size match to
an illustration with no stated scale.

The old hill geometry is removed, not the retained café or original buildings.
No island measurements in square kilometres are inferred from the reference.
